Course Overview

Probability & Statistics

Explore data, uncertainty, and the mathematics behind making informed decisions.

Welcome

This course introduces students to the foundations of probability and statistical thinking. We will analyze patterns in data, model random events, and use mathematics to interpret the world around us.

Units

Unit 1

Unit 1: Thinking Statistically

Learn how to collect, organize, and represent data.

 Unit 2

Unit 2: Exploring Data

Measure center, spread, and overall patterns in data.

 Unit 3

Unit 3: Simulation Inference

Build the rules and language of chance and uncertainty.

 Unit 4

Unit 4: Probability

Explore distributions and expected values in random contexts.

 Unit 5

Unit 5: Applications

Use data to make predictions and draw conclusions.
